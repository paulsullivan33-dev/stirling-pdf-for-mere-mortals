# The Neighbours

**Scope:** the other ways the same
errands get done, and what each
trades. Stirling PDF's bet is
*local, broad, and free*; every
neighbour below beats it somewhere
honest.

| Neighbour | What it is | What it trades |
|---|---|---|
| **Adobe Acrobat** | The original author's own toolkit, subscription | Polish and the deepest editing; your money, and cloud features that want your files |
| **The upload sites** (iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda's web side) | The same shelf in someone else's browser tab | Zero install; your document on their server, under their policy and their limits |
| **Sejda / PDF24 desktop apps** | Downloadable tools for Windows/macOS | Local like Stirling, narrower shelf; PDF24 is Windows-only |
| **qpdf, Ghostscript, PDFtk** | The command-line elders | Scriptable power and precision; no page, no mercy, syntax from another decade |
| **PDF Arranger** | A small visual page-shuffler | Just merge/split/rearrange, beautifully simple; nothing else |
| **LibreOffice Draw** | The office suite's PDF corner | Opens a PDF as drawings you can nudge; reflow it does not |
| **OCR specialists** (OCRmyPDF alone, Tesseract alone) | Chapter 8's engines, unboxed | The best OCR control there is; you bring your own shelf |
| **Paperless-ngx** | A document *archive* with OCR built in | Keeps and finds your documents forever; it files, Stirling fetches and carries |

Three readings of the table:

- **If the errand is occasional and
  the document is public** — a
  council form, a manual — the upload
  sites are genuinely fine, and this
  guide's position is that you should
  know *which* documents those are,
  and notice how few of yours qualify.
- **If you live at the command
  line**, the elders plus Stirling's
  API (chapter 9) cover everything;
  the API exists precisely so the
  shelf can join a script without
  giving up the page your household
  uses.
- **If the documents are the family
  archive**, you want a bench *and*
  shelves: Stirling for the work,
  Paperless or plain folders for the
  keeping. The tools are complements
  wearing similar hats.

And the commercial suites keep one
honest crown: if your living is
documents — prepress, legal
production, certified signatures —
Acrobat and its professional kin do
things this shelf doesn't attempt,
and chapter 15 says so without
flinching.
